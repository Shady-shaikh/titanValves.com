UNI KLINGER UKL HIGH PRESSURE GATE GATE VALVES

Overview

Leader Gate Valves are among the most widely used valves for isolation applications, ensuring uninterrupted flow with minimal pressure drop. Designed for air, gas, steam, water, petrochemicals, and process industries, these valves are built with rugged SG iron construction and conform to BS 5153 standards. Trusted across power plants, refineries, fertilizers, chemical, and steel industries, they guarantee durability, safety, and long service life.

Key Features

  • Bolted bonnet with plug type disc for reliable performance
  • Renewable working parts and full flow area for efficiency
  • Suitable for air, gas, low steam, LPG, chemicals, water, and petrochemicals
  • Available with flanged and butt-weld end connections
  • Body test pressure: 30 Bar (hydraulic) / 6.9 Bar (hydraulic)
  • Sizes: 15 mm to 250 mm with forged steel or cast alloy steel options

UNI KLINGER UKL MAKE PISTON VALVE

Overview

The UKL Piston Valve is a seatless and glandless valve designed to replace conventional gate and globe valves with superior sealing and durability. Built with resilient rings and a stainless-steel piston, it ensures bubble-tight shutoff equivalent or superior to Class VI leak standards. Ideal for air, gas, steam, LPG, chemicals, and petrochemicals, this valve is widely used across power plants, refineries, fertilizers, steel, and process industries.

Key Features

  • Seatless and glandless design for leak-proof performance
  • Stainless steel piston with resilient sealing rings for long service life
  • Bubble-tight shutoff meeting Class VI leak standards
  • Suitable for air, gas, steam, LPG, chemicals, and water applications
  • Size range: 15 NB to 40 NB, with max operating pressure 55 kg/cm² and temperature 427°C
  • End connections: Screwed (BSP/BSPT/NPT), Socket weld, Flanged #150/#300
  • Material options: CF8 / CF8M, IBR and Non-IBR compliant

UNI KLINGER UKL MAKE THERMODYNAMIC STEAM TRAP

Overview

The Thermodynamic Steam Trap operates on the principle of velocity differences between steam and condensate, ensuring efficient condensate discharge while preventing steam loss. Widely used in power plants, refineries, petrochemicals, fertilizers, sugar, and process industries, it is highly reliable for drip lines of main steam distribution systems.

Key Features

  • Works on velocity difference of steam and condensate for efficient operation
  • Prevents live steam loss with self-actuating disc design
  • Compact, sturdy, and suitable for high-pressure applications
  • Size options: 15, 20, and 25 mm
  • Wide material range: AISI 420, ASTM A217 WC6, ASTM A182 F22 Cl3
  • End connections: Threaded (BSP/BSPT/NPT), Socket Weld, Butt Weld, and Flanged (#150–#1500)
  • Handles high temperature: up to 550°C and pressure up to 250 bar
