OVERVIEW

We are one of India’s most trusted suppliers of branded industrial valves, offering a complete range from world-leading manufacturers including KSB, BDK Valves, Audco Valves, Forbes Marshall Valves, Spirax Valves, Uni Klinger Piston Valves, Intervalve Poonawalla Valves, Kirloskar Valves, and Leader Valves.

Our portfolio includes KSB gate valves, KSB ball valves, and KSB valve distributor services in India, ensuring availability across industries. As an authorized channel for Weir BDK Valves, we supply BDK butterfly valves, BDK ball valves, BDK gate valves, BDK diaphragm valves, BDK cast steel globe valves, BDK eccentric butterfly valves, and more, positioning ourselves as a leading BDK valve manufacturer and BDK valve supplier in India.

From the Audco range, we deliver premium-quality Audco butterfly valves, Audco ball valves, Audco plug valves, Audco gate valves, Audco globe valves, Audco lubricated plug valves, and Audco Slimseal butterfly valves. We also stock Serck Audco pressure balanced plug valves and Flowserve Audco valves trusted in demanding applications.

In partnership with Forbes Marshall, we provide precision-engineered solutions such as Forbes Marshall control valves, Forbes Marshall gate valves, Forbes Marshall globe valves, Forbes Marshall ball valves, Forbes Marshall piston valves, Forbes Marshall safety relief valves, Forbes Marshall three-way valves, Forbes Marshall Ecotrol control valves, and the specialized Forbes Marshall PRDS valve.

Our Spirax range includes highly reliable Spirax control valves, Spirax pressure reducing valves, Spirax desuperheating valves, Spirax PRVs, Spirax Spira-trol control valves, Spirax piston valves, Spirax check valves, Spirax safety valves, and Spirax steam control valves—all designed for efficient steam and condensate management.

From Uni Klinger (UKL), we offer advanced solutions such as UKL piston valves, Uni Klinger valves, Uni Klinger safety valves, Uni Klinger bellow seal valves, Uni Klinger steam traps, Uni Klinger control valves, Uni Klinger thermodynamic steam traps, Uni Klinger pressure relief valves, and Uni Klinger trap valve stations.

We are also proud suppliers of Intervalve Poonawalla Valves, including Intervalve butterfly valves, Intervalve IVTEX triple-offset butterfly valves, Intervalve IVGKL rubber lined butterfly valves, Intervalve ball valves, Intervalve forged gate valves, Intervalve cast steel globe valves, Intervalve dual-plate check valves, Intervalve swing check valves, and more, making us a reliable Intervalve valve manufacturer in Pune.

Our Kirloskar range covers heavy-duty solutions such as Kirloskar butterfly valves, Kirloskar sluice (gate) valves, Kirloskar cast steel gate valves, Kirloskar forged steel gate valves, Kirloskar globe valves, Kirloskar ball valves, Kirloskar check valves, Kirloskar air valves, and large-size Kirloskar valves designed for infrastructure and industrial use.

Finally, with Leader Valves, we supply a complete line including Leader ball valves, Leader butterfly valves, Leader gate valves, Leader globe valves, Leader check valves, Leader pressure reducing valves, Leader safety valves, and Leader thermodynamic steam traps, making us a trusted Leader valve manufacturer in India.

With such a vast range of branded industrial valves under one roof, we ensure unmatched availability, quality, and service for every industry.
