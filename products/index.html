Titan Flow Valves offers a comprehensive range of high-performance industrial valve products engineered to meet the demanding requirements of modern process industries. Our product portfolio includes precision-designed ball valves, gate valves, globe valves, strainers, check valves, and butterfly valves—each built to deliver superior reliability, tight shutoff, and long service life in critical applications.

Titan Flow Valves ball valves provide smooth operation and leak-proof performance, making them ideal for on/off control in oil & gas, chemical, and water treatment systems. Our gate valves are designed for full-bore flow and minimal pressure drop, suitable for pipelines and heavy-duty industrial operations. For accurate flow regulation, Titan Flow Valves globe valves deliver exceptional control and durability even under high-pressure or high-temperature conditions.

To protect equipment and maintain system efficiency, our industrial strainers effectively remove debris and contaminants from pipelines. Titan Flow Valves check valves ensure reliable backflow prevention across pumping, process, and utility lines. Additionally, our butterfly valves offer lightweight, cost-effective flow control with excellent sealing performance for HVAC, water distribution, and industrial applications.
