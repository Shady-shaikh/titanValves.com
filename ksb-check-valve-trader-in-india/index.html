Installation and Maintenance Guidelines

To ensure the longevity of your flow control system, proper setup of an NRV is paramount. Our technical team suggests the following:

  1. Flow Direction: Always verify the flow arrow cast into the body matches the actual pipeline flow.
  2. Pipeline Position: While many designs work in horizontal lines, vertical installations require specific spring-loaded or lift-check models to function against gravity.
  3. Upstream Distance: For optimal performance and to reduce turbulence, install the unit at least five pipe diameters away from pumps or elbows.
  4. Regular Inspection: Periodically check for “slamming” sounds, which may indicate a need for a different closure speed or spring tension.
